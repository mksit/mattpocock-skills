The plugin ships a third agent, `fable-advisor`: read-only judgment on Fable, adapted from DannyMac180/fable-advisor (MIT). `to-spec`, `to-tickets`, `grilling` (and so `grill-with-docs`), `diagnosing-bugs` and `implement-spec` consult it at their commitment boundaries, and `implement-spec` ends on its final gate. Fable judges and never implements: the seed routing drops the `judgment` implementer tier, and a `hard` implementer that fails twice waits for the advisor to name the cause.
