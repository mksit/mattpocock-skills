The plugin ships a third agent, `fable-advisor`, a read-only advisor that gives judgment on Fable. It's adapted from DannyMac180/fable-advisor (MIT). `to-spec`, `to-tickets`, `grilling` (and so `grill-with-docs`), `diagnosing-bugs` and `implement-spec` consult it at their commitment boundaries, and `implement-spec` ends on its final gate.

Fable judges and never implements. The seed routing drops the `judgment` implementer tier, and a `hard` implementer that fails twice waits for the advisor to name the cause.

`implement-spec` now runs autonomously to its final gate. Open design choices go to `fable-advisor`, and work found outside the spec becomes tickets. It only stops for an action the repo reserves to the user, a **Rethink**, or a `hard` ticket that failed twice with no named cause.
