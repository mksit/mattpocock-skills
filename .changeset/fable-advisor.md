The plugin ships a third agent, `fable-advisor`: read-only judgment on Fable, adapted from DannyMac180/fable-advisor (MIT). `to-spec`, `to-tickets`, `grilling` (and so `grill-with-docs`), `diagnosing-bugs` and `implement-spec` consult it at their commitment boundaries, and `implement-spec` ends on its final gate. Fable judges and never implements: the seed routing drops the `judgment` implementer tier, and a `hard` implementer that fails twice waits for the advisor to name the cause. `implement-spec` runs autonomously to its final gate: open choices go to `fable-advisor`, outside work becomes tickets, and it stops only for actions the repo reserves to the user, a **Rethink**, or a twice-failed `hard` ticket with no named cause.
